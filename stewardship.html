Stewardship

RPM Habitat Conservation manages its land with as light a hand as possible. The aim is not to reshape the property but to protect it, and to let the trees, plants, and wildlife that live there carry on undisturbed.

Approach

The tract is managed for natural processes rather than for use or appearance. It is not developed, not open to the public, and not offered for recreation or events. Intervention is limited to what is needed to keep the forest intact.

Rare plant conservation

Florida torreya, pure American chestnut, and running buffalo clover are conserved on the property and looked after over time. Their locations and numbers are not published.

Long-term ownership

RPM Habitat Conservation is privately funded and holds the land for the long term, as forest.
