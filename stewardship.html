Stewardship

The work here is physical and ongoing — holding the land, growing what goes back onto it, and keeping a rare conifer going.

Reforestation and native propagation

A small container nursery on the property propagates native trees and shrubs from native stock. Those plantings go back onto the hillside, thickening the canopy where it's thin and adding root structure to ground that needs it.

The rhythm of it follows the seasons: propagation and care through the growing months, planting and slope work once stock goes dormant.

Rare conifer stewardship

Part of the work is simply keeping the tract's Torreya taxifolia specimens alive and healthy where they stand. That's a small but real contribution to the wider assisted-migration conversation around the species — see Torreya Guardians for the broader effort this fits into.

Holding the land

None of the above matters if the tract doesn't stay held. Keeping this hillside in conservation ownership, rather than letting it re-enter the development cycle it fell out of, is the first and ongoing piece of the work.
