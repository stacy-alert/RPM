A forested hillside on the Ohio River, held out of development.

RPM Habitat Conservation, LLC owns a wooded hillside tract in Miami Township, Hamilton County, Ohio, in the hill country above the Ohio River. The land runs steeply down through the Kope Formation — interbedded shale and limestone, notoriously prone to landslide — to the confluence of two creeks at the low point of the tract.

The tract had forfeited to the State of Ohio and went to a Hamilton County tax-forfeited land sale in September 2025. RPM Habitat Conservation bought it there rather than let it return to the development cycle it had fallen out of; the deed was recorded that November. It isn't a park, and it isn't open to the public. It's a working conservation property, held for the long term.
