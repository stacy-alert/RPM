A wooded Ohio River hillside, held for conservation.

RPM Habitat Conservation, LLC is an Ohio limited liability company that holds a wooded hillside tract in Miami Township, Hamilton County, in the hill country above the Ohio River. The land is held for one purpose: to remain forest, and to be left to the plants and wildlife that live there.

The tract is private property and is not open to the public, though private tours can be arranged by contacting us.

The land

Late Ordovician bedrock, a mature forest canopy, and two creeks meeting at the foot of the hill.

About the land

Our approach

Minimal intervention. The forest is protected and left to grow on its own terms.

Rare plants

Florida torreya, pure American chestnut, and running buffalo clover are conserved on the property.
